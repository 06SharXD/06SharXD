### 🔍 Interactive BA Methodology
*Click below to see how I approach business problems:*
<details>
  <summary><b>1. Discovery & Requirements (The "What")</b></summary>
  <ul>
    <li>Conduct stakeholder interviews to define primary business objectives and constraints.</li>
    <li>Author comprehensive <b>Business Requirement Documents (BRDs)</b> and establish Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and statistical success thresholds.</li>
  </ul>
</details>
<details>
  <summary><b>2. Data Engineering & Analysis (The "Why")</b></summary>
  <ul>
    <li>Extract and cleanse operational data using <b>SQL</b> and <b>Python (Pandas)</b>.</li>
    <li>Apply predictive modeling (Scikit-Learn) or statistical testing (A/B testing, SciPy) to uncover hidden bottlenecks.</li>
  </ul>
</details>
<details>
  <summary><b>3. Visualization & Execution (The "How")</b></summary>
  <ul>
    <li>Translate complex model outputs into executive-ready <b>Tableau</b> or <b>Power BI</b> dashboards.</li>
    <li>Map dynamic system workflows in <b>Figma</b> and author <b>Functional Requirement Documents (FRDs)</b> for engineering hand-off.</li>
  </ul>
</details>
